# Delete your GoodBefore data

**Last updated:** 10 November 2026

GoodBefore, published earlier as FreshTrack, is a food expiration tracker by Arkayen Labs. It does not ask you to create an account, and your food list is stored on your phone, not on our servers. This page explains how to delete your data.

## Your food list

Your items, settings and progress are stored only on your phone. To delete them:

- Delete items one by one in the app, or
- Clear GoodBefore's storage in your phone's settings (Settings, Apps, GoodBefore, Storage, Clear storage), or
- Uninstall the app.

If Android's backup to your Google Account was turned on, a copy of the food list may also be in that backup. You can delete it from your Google Account's backups in your phone's settings.

## Usage statistics and crash reports

These are off unless you turned them on. To stop them, turn them off in the app's Settings. Data already collected is not linked to your name or email, is kept under the retention periods in the [Privacy Policy](https://www.arkayenlabs.com/privacy/goodbefore), and is then deleted.

## Accounts created in an earlier version

If you created an account when the app was called FreshTrack, you can delete it in two ways:

- **In the app:** sign in, open Settings, and choose Delete Account. This deletes your account and its data straight away.
- **By email, without the app:** write to support@arkayenlabs.com from the email address on the account, with the subject "Delete my GoodBefore account". We will confirm the request and delete the account and its data within 30 days.

Deleting the account removes your email address, display name and account identifier. We keep nothing else about the account afterwards, apart from the email exchange about the request itself.
